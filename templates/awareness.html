🛡️ Cyber Security Awareness

🎣 Phishing

Attackers use fake messages or websites to trick people into revealing information.

🔗 Suspicious Links

Check the domain carefully before clicking or entering sensitive information.

🔐 Password Security

Use long, unique passwords and avoid reusing them.

🔑 2FA

Two-factor authentication adds another layer of account protection.

🚨 Urgency

Be careful with messages that pressure you to act immediately.

📥 Downloads

Avoid unknown attachments and files from untrusted sources.
